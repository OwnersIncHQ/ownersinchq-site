The problem

The envelope from the IRS — a CP59, the one that says "we have no record of your return" — doesn't arrive because you're careless. It arrives because the paperwork piled up: a year you meant to file, then another, bank statements in one place, W-2s in another, and no clean way to hand any of it to a tax professional.

You're not alone in that pile. The IRS mailed roughly 3.37 million of those nonfiler notices in fiscal 2025, and 2.88 million more in just the first seven months of fiscal 2026. And here's the part nobody tells you: a CPA can't file from a shoebox. Tax preparers bill extra for the mess, surcharge it, or turn it away — because sorting three years of statements isn't filing, it's a whole job before the filing.

Your first problem isn't legal. It's "I can't face the paperwork." That problem is the one we solve.

What this is — and what it isn't

What this is — and what it isn't. Owners Inc. provides bookkeeping and records-organization services. We are not a tax preparation firm, and we are not CPAs, enrolled agents, or attorneys. We do not prepare, sign, or file tax returns. We do not give tax advice, and we cannot represent you before the IRS or the Minnesota Department of Revenue or respond to notices on your behalf.

What we do: we take your paperwork the way it is — boxes, envelopes, email attachments, bank statements — and turn it into an organized, CPA-ready packet: every document sorted and digitized, income and expenses summarized year by year from your records, and a checklist of anything still missing. Your licensed tax professional makes every tax decision and prepares and files your returns. If you don't have one, we'll introduce you to local firms we work with.

About the numbers in your packet: summaries are built from the records you provide and are labeled with what's missing. They are bookkeeping summaries, not tax returns and not tax calculations — your preparer decides how everything is reported. We can't estimate your refund, your balance due, or whether penalties will be removed; anyone who promises you a number before your records are organized is guessing.

Questions

Why don't you just file it for me?

Because filing is a different, credentialed job. Preparing a return for pay requires a federal PTIN and means making the tax decisions — deductions, how income is characterized, what's claimed. We deliberately don't do any of that. We do the part before it: turning your records into something a preparer can file from quickly and accurately. That split is also why CPAs trust sending clients to us — we never compete for the return.

I don't have a CPA.

No problem — it's the common case. We'll introduce you to local firms we work with, and your packet is built to be handed to any preparer you choose. The organizing work is yours either way; nothing about the packet locks you to one firm.

Some of my paperwork is just gone.

That's expected, and it's part of what the Records Review maps. Your packet includes a missing-documents checklist that names exactly what's absent — and for records like wage transcripts, your CPA can pull them from the IRS. We work from the records that exist and label every gap, so your preparer is never guessing about what's missing.

How many years do I need to deal with?

The IRS generally wants the last six years of returns filed before it treats a nonfiler as back in compliance. Most people who come to us are behind two or three years. The Records Review tells you exactly which years have paperwork and which don't — that's the starting map.

What will I owe? Will I get a refund?

Nobody can honestly answer that before your records are organized — anyone who quotes you a number first is guessing. What we can tell you up front is our fee, in writing. What you owe or get back is your preparer's calculation, made from the packet we build.

What does the whole thing cost, start to finish?

Two fees, from two different professionals: ours for the organizing (tiers above — the $149 Records Review is credited if you sign within 30 days), and your tax preparer's fee for preparing and filing the returns. No hourly billing from us, no surprises — one fixed number in writing before any work starts.

I already bought the Back-Taxes Organizer PDF. Is this the same thing?

Same system, two ways to run it. The $24 Organizer is the do-it-yourself version — checklists and structure for sorting your own shoebox. Tax Chaos Cleanup is the done-for-you version: we do the sorting, digitizing, and summarizing, and hand you the finished packet.
