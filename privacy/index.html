Privacy & Terms

What we collect, what we never do with it, and how to get removed. Written in plain language, because the fine print should not need a translator.

Owners Inc. Everybody's doing it.

Owners Inc. may earn a commission on purchases through our links — it never changes your price.

Last updated Nov 24, 2026. This page covers ownersinchq.com — the deals site, the bookkeeping pages, the email list, and the chat box.

What we collect

  • Email list (Kit). When you join the list — for the deal sheet or the bookkeeping checklist — you give us your name and your email address, and we note which of those two things you asked for. That's all. Double opt-in is on: you confirm by email before you get anything.
  • Bookkeeping leads (the chat box). When you ask about bookkeeping in the chat on the bookkeeping page, you give us your name, your email, and roughly how far behind your books are (the range you pick). That goes to our inbox so we can answer you and send the written quote we promised. Nothing else is taken.
  • Emailing us directly. If you email us, we get whatever you put in the message, and we reply to it. Same as any email you'd send to anyone.
  • Site visits (Google Analytics). We count visits and see which pages people read, in aggregate — what was looked at, not who looked at it. We don't run ads that follow you around, and we don't buy visitor data.

What we never do

  • We never sell your information. Not your email, not your name, not your lead details — not to anyone, for any price. We never rent or trade it either.
  • We never share it except to run the thing you asked for. The only outside hands it passes through are the services that make the list and the lead form work — Kit (the email list) and FormSubmit (the chat form) — and they hold it only to deliver what you signed up for.
  • We never put affiliate links in email. Our deal emails link to our own pages, never straight to a store's affiliate link.
  • We never use your lead details for anything else. A bookkeeping inquiry becomes a bookkeeping conversation, not a deal email and not a sales list — unless you separately join the list yourself.

Affiliate disclosure

Owners Inc. is an affiliate page. If you buy through a link on this site, Owners Inc. may earn a commission — and it costs you nothing extra; the price is the same as going straight to the store. The store pays Owners Inc. a commission out of its own pocket for sending you — that's the whole affiliate deal, plain and simple. When you click out to a store, that store's own privacy policy takes over from there; what you do on their site is between you and them.

Your choices — getting removed

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list.
  • Want your details deleted, not just unsubscribed? Email hello@ownersinchq.com with the subject "Delete my info" and the email address you used, and we'll remove it and confirm back to you. The same goes for a bookkeeping lead — ask and it's gone.
  • You can also call us at (651) 314-9387.

Terms — using the site

  • Prices and availability are checked on the dates shown and can change. The retailer's page is always the final word. We never invent a price, a discount, or a rating.
  • The site is information, not advice. A deal page is a deal page; it is not financial advice. Bookkeeping pages describe a service; the service itself starts only with a signed engagement letter, and only then — a quote or a chat answer is not a contract.
  • Content is ours. The writing, the logo painting, and the videos on this site are Owners Inc.'s. Share them with a link back; don't repost them as your own.
  • We're a family-owned business in Hastings, Minnesota. We are not a corporation; "Owners Inc." is the business's name, not a claim about how it's incorporated.
  • Changes. If this page changes in a way that matters, the "last updated" date at the top changes with it.

Questions
