How signup bonuses work

Banks and brokerages sometimes offer a bonus for opening a new account and using it the way they ask. We don’t print bonus amounts we can’t verify — they change too fast — but the run of it is the same almost everywhere:

1. Open the account

You apply with the bank or brokerage and open the account named in their current offer.

2. Do what they ask

That might be a minimum deposit, a couple of direct deposits, or a set number of debit card purchases — whatever their offer spells out.

3. The bonus lands later

Once you’ve met the requirements and the waiting period passes, the bonus lands in your account.

The exact bonus amounts, rates, and rules come from each provider’s own current offer, so always read that offer before you open anything. And nobody here can promise you’ll be approved — that’s each provider’s call, not ours.
