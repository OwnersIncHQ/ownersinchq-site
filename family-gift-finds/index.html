How we pick these gifts

This page is built the way families actually shop: by the person, not by the product category. Every find is a live listing at a named store with the price and check date on it, and we lean toward gifts that still get used after the first week — things for the kitchen table, the yard, the car, and the couch, not novelties that end up in a drawer.

What to watch for: in December the deal is only half the job — the other half is the box arriving in time. Check the retailer's delivery estimate on the listing before you buy, especially in the last two weeks before Christmas; our Christmas page tracks the order-by deadlines store by store. And keep the order email until the gift lands: prices move in both directions this season, and some stores will adjust a price that drops inside their own window if you ask with the order number in hand.
