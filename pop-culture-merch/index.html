How we pick this merch

The finds here are licensed gear and collectibles from the shows, games, and teams people actually ask about — each one a live listing at a named retailer, priced and date-stamped when we checked it. Merch is where the check date matters most: licensed runs sell through sizes, and a listing that was fully stocked on Monday can be down to one size by the weekend.

What to watch for: if the design is the point, check that the listing names the brand or licensor — official gear says so, and the print and fabric on licensed pieces hold up better than the knockoffs that copy the artwork. On apparel, read the size chart on the listing instead of guessing; fan merch cuts vary by maker. Buying a gift? Buy early in the run — once a popular size sells out, an exchange may not be there when you need it.
