Deadlines coming up — why December matters

Tax season runs on fixed dates, and clean books have to come first. Your tax preparer can only file from books that are ready — so the cleanup needs to happen before the preparer starts, not while a deadline is already here.

March 15 — the real deadline

S-corporation and partnership returns are due March 15. A tax preparer can't file from messy books — your books need to be clean before the preparer starts.

January 31 — 1099s are due

1099-NEC forms are due January 31. If you paid contractors during the year, those payments need to be sorted before then, so the forms can go out on time.

QuickBooks Desktop 2023 — time to move

QuickBooks Desktop 2023 lost support, payroll, bank feeds, and payments on May 31, 2026. If you are still on it, you have to migrate — and a messy file needs cleanup as part of the move. We handle the cleanup side of that migration.

Book in December

Cleanup slots booked in December are the ones guaranteed finished before tax season. January bookings are taken in order and can't be guaranteed finished before the deadlines above.
